# Module 15: Design Patterns for Android

## Module Overview
"Design Patterns" are reusable solutions to common problems.
You might know definitions ("A Singleton is a class with one instance"), but do you know *why* `ContextWrapper` is a Decorator? Or why `RecyclerView` uses an Adapter?
In this module, we map the classic **Gang of Four (GoF)** patterns directly to the **Android SDK**.
We learn not just how to implement them, but how to recognize them in the libraries we use every day.

## What You Will Learn
- **Creational**: Why `NotificationCompat.Builder` exists and why `ViewModelProvider` is a Factory.
- **Structural**: How `Retrofit` is a Facade and `RecyclerView.Adapter` is... well, an Adapter.
- **Behavioral**: How `LiveData` is the Observer pattern and `DiffUtil` is the Strategy pattern.
- **Architectural**: The patterns that structure entire apps (Repository, DI).
