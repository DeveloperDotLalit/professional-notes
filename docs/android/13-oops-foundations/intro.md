## Module Overview
Object-Oriented Programming is not just about syntax; it's about **Modeling Reality**.
This module strips away the complexity of frameworks and frameworks to focus on the raw building blocks of modern software: Objects, State, and Behavior.
We go beyond the textbook definitions of "Encapsulation" and dive into the **Memory Models**, **Virtual Tables**, and **Design Trade-offs** that distinguish a Junior Developer from a Staff Engineer.

## What You Will Learn

- **The Physical Reality**: How Objects are allocated on the Heap vs Stack.
- **The Polymorphic cost**: How `v-tables` enable dynamic dispatch and why it costs CPU cycles.
- **The Inheritance Trap**: Why "Is-A" relationships are often a mistake and how to use Composition.
- **Kotlin Superpowers**: How `data class`, `object`, and `delegation` simplify OOPS.

## Prerequisites

- Basic functions and variables (Module 01).
- No Android knowledge required.
