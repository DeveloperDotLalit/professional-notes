# Syllabus Overview

## 🎓 Course Philosophy
This course is modeled after a rigorous University-level Computer Science elective, combined with the practical demands of a Senior Android Engineer role at a top-tier tech company. We move beyond *tutorials* and focus on *engineering*.

## 🔑 Key Learning Outcomes

### 1. Master the Language (Kotlin)
You will stop writing "Java in Kotlin" and start writing idiomatic Kotlin. You'll understand inline classes, reified types, and how coroutines map to state machines.

### 2. Own the Platform (Android)
You will understand the Android OS. Not just `onCreate`, but how the `ActivityThread` works, how `Binder` transactions operate, and how the `Choreographer` handles frames.

### 3. Architect for Scale
You will learn Clean Architecture, not as a template, but as a philosophy. You will understand how to manage dependencies, abstract data sources, and keep your UI dumb.

### 4. Concurrency without Fear
Coroutines and Flows will become second nature. You will handle race conditions, structured concurrency, and complex stream transformations with confidence.
