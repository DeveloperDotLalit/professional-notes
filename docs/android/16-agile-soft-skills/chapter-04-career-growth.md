## 1. Task Doer vs Engineer
**Junior**: "I finished the ticket."
**Senior**: "I delivered the feature and verified it in production."
**Ownership**: It means you care about the outcome. You check the crash logs after release. You talk to the celebrity users. You don't just throw code over the wall to QA.

## 2. Disagree and Commit
In meetings, argue your point passionately.
"I think Architecture A is better because..."
But if the team decides on Architecture B: **Commit**.
Do not sabotage it. Do not say "I told you so" if it fails.
Full support for the team decision is a hallmark of maturity.

## 3. Improving the Process
Don't just complain about things ("Deployments are slow").
Fix them.
"I wrote a script to automate deploy. It saves us 20 mins a day."
This is **High Impact** work. This gets you promoted.
Solve problems for *your team*, not just for the product.
